mail-agent – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

בקצרה: mail-agent רץ על המחשב שלך בלבד. הוא קורא מיילים חדשים מתיבת ה־Gmail שלך, שולח את תוכנם ל־Google Gemini כדי לנסח טיוטות תשובה, ושולח תשובה רק אחרי שאישרת אותה. אין שרת של המפתחת, והמידע לא נמכר ולא מועבר לאף אחד אחר.

1. Who we are

mail-agent is a personal email-reply assistant developed by Lea Fruchter. It runs entirely on the user's own computer. There is no mail-agent server: the developer does not receive, see or store any of your data.

2. What data the app accesses

With your permission (Google OAuth scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.modify), the app:

  • Reads recent messages in your inbox: sender, subject, date, message and thread identifiers, and message body.
  • Sends replies from your account, only after you approve each draft on the local review page.

The app does not delete messages, change labels, read your contacts or access any other Google service.

3. How the data is used

  • Filtering: newsletters, automated messages and your own messages are skipped locally, without being sent anywhere.
  • Drafting: for the remaining messages, a shortened excerpt (sender, subject and up to about 2,500 characters of text) is sent to the Google Gemini API to decide whether a reply is needed and to write a draft.
  • Sending: a reply is sent through the Gmail API only after you approve it.

Data is used only to provide these features to you. It is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used by the developer to train AI models.

4. Third parties

Email excerpts are processed by Google Gemini API (Google LLC) under Google's Gemini API terms. On Google's unpaid (free) tier, Google states that submitted content may be used to improve its products and may be reviewed by humans. Users who do not want this should use a paid Gemini API tier. No other third party receives your data.

5. Storage and retention

  • All app data is stored only on your computer, in the app's data folder: drafts and short excerpts of the original messages (SQLite database), your Google sign-in token (token.json), and logs.
  • The data stays there until you delete it. Deleting the data folder removes everything the app stored.

6. Your choices

  • You can revoke the app's access to Gmail at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • You decide on every draft: approve, reject with a comment, or leave it for the next day. Nothing is sent without your approval.

7. Google API Services User Data Policy

mail-agent's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
